Add a shortcut on iPhone

These are custom shortcuts. After you tap Get Shortcut, iOS may ask you to allow shortcuts from other people.

1. Open Settings Go to Shortcuts → Advanced and turn on Allow Untrusted Shortcuts if iOS asks for it.
2. Tap Get Shortcut That opens the official iCloud page. Review the actions, then tap Add Shortcut.
3. Run it Find it in the Shortcuts app. You can also pin it to your Home Screen or ask Siri to run it.
